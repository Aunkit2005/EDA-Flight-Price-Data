# ✈️ Flight Price Data Analysis  

## 📖 Project Overview  
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Flight Price dataset, with the goal of uncovering how different factors (such as departure time, airline, route, stops, and date) influence flight ticket prices. Through cleaning, visualization, and analysis, the aim is to derive actionable insights and understand what drives price variation in air travel.

## 🧰 Tools & Libraries  
- **Python** – for data manipulation and logic  
- **Pandas** – data loading, cleaning and aggregation  
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – visualising distributions, trends and relationships  

## 📊 Dataset Details  
- **Name:** Flight Price Prediction  
- **Source:** Kaggle  
- **Contents:** Records of flights with features like airline, source/destination, departure/arrival times, stops, and ticket price  
- **Purpose:** Understand and visualize factors that drive flight ticket pricing  

## 🔍 Key Analysis Steps  
1. Load the dataset, inspect schema, and identify missing or inconsistent data.  
2. Clean and preprocess: handle missing values, convert date/time features, encode categorical variables.  
3. Explore individual features: distribution of ticket prices, number of stops, airlines, routes.  
4. Perform bivariate & multivariate analysis: e.g., price vs number of stops, price vs route, price vs airline.  
5. Visualise insights: histograms, box plots, scatter plots , bar plot, pie chart
6. Summarise findings and draw conclusions about which factors tend to increase or decrease flight prices.  

## 🎯 Outcome & Insights  
By the end of this project you will gain a clearer view of how flight ticket pricing is affected by various operational and travel features. These insights could be useful for travellers, airlines and analysts alike in understanding pricing strategy or making travel decisions.
